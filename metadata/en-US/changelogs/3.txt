v0.2.0

New:
- “On this day” widget: a photo taken on this day in earlier years
- “Frame” widget: photos from an album of your choice, changing every hour, every 6 hours or every day

Fixes:
- Tapping the Straighten or Adjust sliders no longer makes the value jump back
- Albums no longer lose their items when an SD card is removed or files are rescanned
- Opening the map no longer freezes the app
- Settings › Language now marks the selected language right away
