v0.1.0

First version:
- Photos and videos on a timeline by day, with the town where each was taken
- Ultra HDR photos and HDR videos shown in HDR
- Your own albums, folders as sources, Favorites and Trash
- Places and a world map from data built into the app, with no Internet access
- Search by date, place, album, type and file name
- Sharing without location by default
- Crop, rotate and straighten, saved as a copy or over the original
- Slideshow and an encrypted vault
