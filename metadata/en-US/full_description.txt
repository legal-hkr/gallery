Gallery shows the photos and videos on your phone and is designed to be the only gallery you need. It has no Internet permission: places, the map and search use data built into the app, and nothing about you leaves the phone.

Photos and videos:
- A timeline by day, in each photo’s own time zone, with the town it was taken in; pinch to change the number of columns
- Ultra HDR photos and HDR videos (HDR10, HLG, Dolby Vision) shown in HDR
- Motion photos, GIFs and animated WebP images play; RAW (DNG), HEIC and AVIF files open
- Zoom to the photo’s full resolution; videos with frame-by-frame stepping, playback speeds and sound only when you want it
- Info with the date and time (editable), the place with a small map, the camera and lens

Albums:
- Your own albums, with an item in as many of them as you like
- Each folder as a source: shown in Photos, only as an album, or hidden
- Favorites, Videos, Screenshots and Trash

Places and search:
- Places by country and town, and a world map with clusters – from Wikidata and Natural Earth data built into the app, nothing downloaded
- Search by date, place, album, type and file name, in English and Polish

Sharing and privacy:
- Shared files have their location removed by default, with a switch right in the share sheet
- A vault encrypted on the phone, unlocked with your fingerprint or screen lock, with import from and export to any storage

Widgets:
- On this day: a photo taken on this day in earlier years
- Frame: photos from an album of your choice, changing every hour, every 6 hours or every day

And also:
- An editor to crop, rotate and straighten, adjust light and color or apply one of six filters, draw and write on photos, and trim videos frame-accurately; edits are saved as a copy or over the original, keeping Ultra HDR, HDR video and metadata
- A slideshow with crossfade or Ken Burns
- Opens photos and videos from other apps, and picks them for other apps
- Backup and restore of settings and albums
- English and Polish, independent of the system language
- Material 3 Expressive design with your phone’s colors
